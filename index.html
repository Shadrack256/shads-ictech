About SHADS-ICTECH

Built in Kampala, for Kampala

SHADS-ICTECH was founded by Ssebagerek Shadrack with one goal: make quality ICT services affordable and accessible to everyday Ugandans — students, shop owners, schools and small organisations.

We are a mobile-first service. Instead of asking you to come to us, we come to your home, office or school with the tools and the patience to get the job done properly.

Integrity first
